1. Research Background & Motivation

The U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC) LEED v5 shifts green building certification from traditional energy cost savings toward whole-life decarbonization (accounting for 50% of total points). Quantifying cradle-to-gate (Modules A1–A3) structure and enclosure embodied carbon is now a mandatory prerequisite (MRp2), while MRc2 rewards 10%, 20%, 30%, and 40% carbon reductions against baseline buildings with 1 to 4 credit points. Furthermore, EAp1 & EAc1 mandate early operational carbon projections and incentivize on-site renewable energy (Rooftop Solar PV).

Up to 80% of embodied carbon reduction potential is locked in during Schematic Design (LOD 100–200). However, traditional workflows require exporting Revit schedules to Excel (~16 hours per iteration with a 30% conversion error rate) or exporting gbXML files that frequently fail due to unenclosed early-stage thermal zones.

2. Four Schematic Design (LOD 100–200) Bottlenecks & CarboRevit Solutions

Early-Stage Bottleneck (LOD 100–200) Why Traditional BIM Workflows Fail CarboRevit Technical Solution
1. Missing 3D Rebar Geometry
(30%–40% Carbon Omission)		 Schematic BIM models only contain rough concrete volumes; 3D reinforcing bars are not modeled until LOD 350+, causing severe underestimation of RC carbon. Parametric Rebar Ratio Imputation: Embeds standard structural rebar densities (Column 150, Beam 130, Slab 95 kg/m³) in the database to automatically compute combined concrete + steel carbon.
2. Unspecified Generic Materials
(Material Uncertainty)		 Architects use generic default Revit materials during massing without specific manufacturer EPDs or LEED v5 baseline references. Zero-Template Category Mapping & What-If Swap: Maps BuiltInCategory directly to LEED v5 regional baselines and enables 1-click swaps (High-SCM Slag Concrete, EAF Rebar, CLT Mass Timber).
3. Broken gbXML Energy Exports
(Unenclosed Thermal Zones)		 Frequent massing iterations and unenclosed rooms cause gbXML exports to external BEM engines (IES-VE, EnergyPlus) to fail. Direct API Envelope & Solar PV Heuristic Engine: Extracts wall, window, and roof areas directly via Revit API to compute WWR, EUI, and rooftop Solar PV green energy offsets in under 3 seconds.
4. Blind Design Iteration
(No Spatial Hotspot Feedback)		 Spreadsheet totals fail to inform architects which specific 3D building elements drive carbon spikes. In-Viewport 3D Carbon Heatmap: Uses OverrideGraphicSettings (Röck et al., 2018) to color-code top 20% carbon hotspots in Red, middle 50% in Amber, and lowest 30% in Emerald Green.

4. Key Questions for Advisor Discussion

  • Research Scope Weighting: Focusing ~80% of the thesis on Phase 1 (Schematic Design LOD 200 Embodied Carbon MRp2/MRc2, parametric rebar imputation, 3D Heatmap, and What-If material swap) with Phase 2 (Operational Green Energy EAp1/EAc1) as a complementary module.
  • Database Architecture: Using the curated local CSV baseline (leed_v5_carbon_baseline.csv combining CLF/EC3 & local ABRI factors) for offline reliability during the MVP stage.
  • Element Scope: Targeting the top 6 Revit categories (Floors, Beams, Columns, Walls, Glazing, Roofs) representing >80% of building carbon impact.
  • Plugin Implementation Framework: Choosing between our completed pyRevit (Python) prototype vs. compiled C# .NET (IExternalCommand) add-in for final demonstration.
