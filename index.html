1. Finalized Computational Task
Given an early-stage (LOD 200) Autodesk Revit BIM model or exported Revit category quantity schedule (CSV) together with user-selected decarbonization parameters (SCM concrete %, EAF recycled rebar %, CLT mass timber swap %, and rooftop solar PV coverage %), the system validates the schema and units, imputes missing LOD 200 structural reinforcement weight (kg/m³), calculates upfront embodied carbon (LEED v5 MRp2/MRc2 A1–A3) and operational solar PV carbon offset (EAp1/EAc1) against a decoupled CLF/ABRI baseline database, and produces a live LEED v5 credit scorecard (0–4 points), a 3D category carbon hotspot heatmap (Red/Amber/Emerald), and an exportable compliance report CSV.
Target Users: Architects, BIM Coordinators, and LEED AP Sustainability Consultants during Schematic Design (LOD 200).
Significant System Integrations: (1) BIM CSV Schedule Import/Export & Schema Validation, (2) Decoupled LEED v5 Carbon Factor Database (leed_v5_carbon_factors.csv), and (3) Autodesk Revit API / pyRevit script integration (FilteredElementCollector & OverrideGraphicSettings).

10. Major Technical Risks, Limitations & Mitigations
1. Unmodeled 3D Rebar at LOD 200: Schematic BIM models omit 3D reinforcement (~35% of structural carbon). Mitigation: Parametric rebar density imputation (95–150 kg/m³) adjustable via Slider #4.
2. Non-Standard Revit Family Naming & Imperial Units: Revit internally stores volume in ft³ and area in ft². Mitigation: Category-level mapping via BuiltInCategory and strict schema/unit validation before calculation.
3. Scope Limitation (Upfront A1–A3 + Operational EAp1): Focuses on the 6 primary structural and envelope categories (>80% of building carbon); MEP systems and interior finishes (B/C stages) are excluded in the MVP.
Stage 3 Requirement: AI / Agent Development Record
AI Tools Used: Google Antigravity Coding Agent & LLM-assisted code generation.
Delegated vs. Student-Directed: AI assisted with Tailwind UI scaffolding, CSV parser boilerplate, and Mermaid diagram syntax; students directed the LEED v5 MRp2/MRc2 formulas, Taiwan ABRI / Taipower (0.494 kgCO₂e/kWh) factors, and LOD 200 rebar imputation ratios.
Significant AI Error Caught & Corrected: Early AI-generated calculation logic computed concrete volume carbon (Volume × Concrete_GWP) without accounting for embedded reinforcing steel and mixed up Revit internal cubic feet (ft³) with cubic meters (m³), underestimating structural carbon by over 35%. We discovered this by cross-checking against our 8-story RC manual Excel benchmark (1,248.4 tCO₂e), verified the unit conversion (1 ft³ = 0.0283168 m³), and added the explicit RebarRatio_kg_m3 × Rebar_GWP term.
